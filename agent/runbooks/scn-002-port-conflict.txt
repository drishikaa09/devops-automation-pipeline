Incident: Service failed to start with an error like "Address already
in use", "EADDRINUSE", or "bind: address already in use" during
service startup or deployment.

Cause: Another process — often a stale container from a previous
failed deployment — is already holding the port the service needs.

Resolution: Use resolve_port_conflict to identify the process holding
the port, verify it is positively identifiable as a stale deployment
of ours, and only then terminate it and restart the service. If
ownership cannot be positively confirmed, do not terminate anything —
this could kill an unrelated process on the runner.

Verification: Confirm the stale owner was terminated, the port is
free, the service binds successfully, its health check passes, and
the originally failed stage is rerun and succeeds.

Notes: If ownership validation fails at any point — even mid-retry —
escalate immediately rather than waiting for the retry budget to run
out. A retry should always revalidate ownership before acting, never
assume the first check still holds.
