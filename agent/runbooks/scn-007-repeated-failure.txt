Incident: Either an individual incident has exhausted its own
scenario's retry or rollback budget without a verified success, or the
same underlying error signature has recurred across multiple separate
incidents within a short time window — even if no single incident
technically ran out of its own retry cap.

Cause: A remediation is not actually holding. A fix that "succeeds"
once but keeps recurring is not resolving the underlying problem.

Resolution: No new fix is attempted — by definition this scenario only
exists because scenario-specific remediation already failed or was
exhausted. Compile the full attempt history (actions tried, order,
verification results for each) into the escalation payload.

Verification: Not applicable — no action is taken to verify.

Notes: This is a circuit breaker, not a fix. Always escalates, with no
further retry path by construction. If an associated runbook_id keeps
getting selected and keeps failing to hold, flag that runbook for
review — the runbook itself may be wrong or incomplete, not just that
one incident was unlucky.
